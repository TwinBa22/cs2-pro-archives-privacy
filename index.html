CS2 Pro Archives Uploader — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

CS2 Pro Archives Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channel CS2 Pro Players GamePlay. It uploads Counter-Strike 2 videos that the owner produced on their own computer to that channel.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube upload (youtube.upload): to upload the owner's videos, as private videos, and set their title, description, tags and thumbnail.
  • YouTube read-only (youtube.readonly): to read the channel's name and its video list, to confirm an upload worked and to avoid uploading the same video twice.

How data is used and stored

  • The app runs only on the channel owner's own computer. There is no server, no website account and no other user.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored only on that computer, encrypted with Windows DPAPI, and is used only to talk to the YouTube Data API.
  • No Google or YouTube data is sold, shared with anyone, used for advertising, or used to train AI models.
  • Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time at Google Account → Third-party access, or by running cs2archive youtube revoke on the owner's computer, which also deletes the stored token.

Contact
